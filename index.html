Tallio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Tallio is a shared expense-tracking app for two people. This page explains what data Tallio handles and how it is used.

Data Tallio stores

Tallio stores the information you enter to track shared expenses:

  • Expense and settlement records (amount, category, date, an optional comment, and who paid)
  • Recurring expense/settlement rules you set up
  • Categories and budgets you create
  • Display names for the two people using the shared household

How your data is stored and synced

Tallio has no backend server operated by the developer. All data is stored using Apple's CloudKit, in your own iCloud account, and synced directly between the two members of a shared household using Apple's CloudKit Sharing. Your data is never sent to the developer or to any third party. Access to it is governed by your Apple ID and Apple's own privacy and security practices.

If you don't enable iCloud sync, your data stays only on your device.

Purchases

Tallio offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock Pro features. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit — Tallio never receives or stores your payment details.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Tallio does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not collect usage data, and your activity in the app is not shared with anyone.

Siri, Shortcuts, and widgets

Support for Siri, Shortcuts, and home-screen widgets works entirely on-device using Apple's frameworks. No expense data leaves your device or iCloud account through these features.

Data deletion

You can delete individual records at any time from within the app. To remove all data, delete the app and, if you wish, remove its data from iCloud via Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your device.

Children's privacy

Tallio is not directed at children and the developer does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
